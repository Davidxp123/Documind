# NYC DOB ALT2 Compliance Guide — Applicable Local Laws by Workflow Stage
### Building-Only Scope | DOB ALT2 Filings | Licensing & Permitting Compliance
*Compiled from official NYC.gov / DOB sources, September 2026*

> **Master index used for verification:** DOB's own "Local Laws" reference page lists every Local Law DOB administers, with a direct link to the enacted text for each:
> **https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/local-laws.page**
> Every citation below was cross-checked against that page and, where possible, an official DOB service notice or program page. Local Law numbers **reset every calendar year** (there is a "Local Law 154 of 2017" *and* a completely different "Local Law 154 of 2021") — always cite the **year** together with the number.

---

## How to Use This Document

This is organized the same way your ALT2 job actually moves through DOB: **Pre-Filing → Filing/Plan Review → Permit Issuance → Construction/LPI → Completion/Sign-Off**, plus a section on **ongoing post-completion obligations** that an ALT2 scope can trigger. For each stage, the table gives:
- The Local Law (number + year)
- What it actually requires
- **When it applies to an ALT2 job specifically** (many of these are broader laws that only bite under certain ALT2 scopes)
- The official source link

## Stage 1 — Pre-Filing / Design Compliance

| Local Law | Subject | Applies to ALT2 when… | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 76 of 1985 / Article 106 (Asbestos Control Program)**, updated by **LL 37 of 2009** | Requires an asbestos investigation before any permit is issued if the work will disturb building material in a building predating April 1, 1987; abatement (if needed) must be DEP-supervised | **Almost always** — every PW1 for a pre-1987 building must state whether the scope requires abatement, doesn't require it, or is exempt (ACP5/ACP7/ACP9/ACP21 process with DEP) | LL37/2009: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll37of2009.pdf · DOB Asbestos Requirements page: https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/dob/project-requirements-asbestos.page |
| **Local Law 106 of 2019** | Requires the Tenant Protection Plan (TPP) to be prepared and submitted by an RDP **retained by the General Contractor**; governs construction-document/applicant-owner statement submittals | Any ALT2 in a building with **occupied dwelling units** that will remain occupied during construction | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll106of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 154 of 2017** | Establishes the modern Tenant Protection Plan (TPP1) content requirements — means/methods for safety, lead/asbestos compliance statement, essential-services continuity, notice posting/distribution, 72-hour advance notice to DOB before starting work | Same trigger as above — TPP1 form filing | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll154of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 118 of 2019** | Requires the TPP to identify the **specific occupied units**, and creates penalties for false statements about tenant occupancy on construction documents | Same trigger as above | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll118of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 116 of 2019** | Requires **heightened review** of TPPs and increased Building Code enforcement around them | Same trigger as above | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll116of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 1 of 2018** | Requires a **Certification of No Harassment (CONH)** before approval of construction documents or permit issuance for demolition/renovation of certain buildings (SRO, rent-regulated, specific districts) | ALT2 scope in a building/district on the CONH pilot list (Hudson Yards, Clinton, Garment Center, SRO buildings, etc. — see HPD1–HPD4 forms) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll1of2018.pdf |
| **Local Law 140 of 2021** & **Local Law 119 of 2021** | Amend/extend the Certification of No Harassment pilot program | Same trigger as above | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll140of2021.pdf · https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll119of2021.pdf |
| **Local Law 40 of 2021** | Lead paint & construction work — improves tenant notification, interagency cooperation, inspections, and DOB's ability to issue Stop Work Orders where lead-paint rules are violated | ALT2 in a pre-1960 (or 1960–1978 presumed) residential building with a lead-paint condition | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll40of2021.pdf |

## Stage 2 — Filing & Plan Review Compliance

| Local Law | Subject | Applies to ALT2 when… | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 48 of 2020**, most recently updated by **Local Law 47 of 2026** | Conforms the **NYC Energy Conservation Code (NYCECC)** to the NY State Energy Code with NYC-specific amendments | Any ALT2 scope touching the building envelope, HVAC, lighting, or service hot water — energy-code compliance documentation (TR8, EN1/EN2, PC1) is a required filing item | LL48/2020: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll48of2020.pdf · LL47/2026 (current version): https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll47of2026.pdf |
| **Local Law 126 of 2021** ("2022 Construction Codes Revision") | Updates the Building, Mechanical, Plumbing, and Fuel Gas Codes (effective Nov. 7, 2022); also re-based special inspection agency/inspector qualification rules (1 RCNY 101-06) | Governs which **special inspections** your ALT2 scope must schedule and who is qualified to perform them | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/int_no_2261-A-2021.pdf |
| **Local Law 33 of 2018** | Requires an **Energy Efficiency Score/Grade** to be posted for covered buildings | Indirectly relevant — DOB/plan examiners may flag if the building's grade record is out of date when filing systems work | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll33of2018.pdf |
| **Local Law 84 of 2009** (Benchmarking) | Requires annual energy/water benchmarking for covered buildings | Not filed *with* the ALT2, but DOB/plan examiners and lenders often check the building is current before approving system-altering work | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll84of2009.pdf |
| **Local Law 87 of 2009** | Requires periodic **energy audits and retro-commissioning** of base building systems | Same — relevant context if the ALT2 involves HVAC/lighting/envelope retrofits | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll87of2009.pdf |
| **Local Law 97 of 2019** (Climate Mobilization Act) | Sets building emissions limits for covered buildings starting 2024, with reporting under Article 320/321 of the Admin. Code | If the ALT2 is part of a broader retrofit intended to bring the building into LL97 compliance, the Professional Attestation Forms for Article 320/321 must accompany filing | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll97of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 105 of 2019** & **Local Law 108 of 2019** | Create sanctions for design professionals who submit **incorrect or false Professional Certification** filings | Directly governs the Professional Certification track most ALT2 jobs use (POC1/PC1) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll105of2019.pdf · https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll108of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 107 of 2019** | Requires DOB to identify and flag unlawful/false statements in submissions | Same context — plan-review integrity checks on the PW1/plan set | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll107of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 154 of 2021** ("Building Electrification Law") | Prohibits on-site combustion of fuels emitting ≥25kg CO2/MMBtu | **Generally does NOT apply to a standard ALT2** — it is scoped to New Building (NB) and Alt-CO "New Building with Existing Elements to Remain" filings only. Relevant only if your alteration is large enough to be reclassified as a "gut renovation"/Alt-CO rather than a true ALT2 | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll154of2021.pdf · DOB program page: https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/codes/ll154-building-electrification.page |

## Stage 3 — Permit Issuance Compliance (Licensing / Contractor Requirements)

| Local Law | Subject | Applies to ALT2 when… | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 196 of 2017** | Establishes **Site Safety Training (SST)** requirements — 40 hrs for workers, 62 hrs for supervisors — on any site that requires a Site Safety Plan / Construction Superintendent / Site Safety Coordinator or Manager | Larger ALT2 jobs that trip the Site Safety Plan threshold (generally major buildings, or work involving sidewalk sheds on tall buildings, per DOB's site-safety criteria) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll196of2017.pdf · DOB Training page: https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/safety/training.page |
| **Local Law 81 of 2017**, amended by **Local Law 149 of 2021** | Requires a **Registered Construction Superintendent** (and an alternate) at certain construction sites, tied to the same Site Safety Plan trigger as LL196 | Same trigger as above | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll81of2017.pdf · https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll149of2021.pdf |
| **Local Law 219 of 2019** | Clarifies who exactly needs SST and who qualifies as an approved SST provider | Same trigger as above | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll1219of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 158 of 2017** | Amends requirements around **construction work permits** generally (PW2 process) | Governs the mechanics of permit issuance for any ALT2 | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll158of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 155 of 2017** | Requires increased oversight of contractors who have previously performed **work without a required permit** | Applies if your registered contractor (or the property) has a prior Work-Without-Permit history | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll155of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 156 of 2017** | Increases civil penalties for **Work Without a Permit** | Relevant across the whole build — sets the penalty structure you're trying to avoid | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll156of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 160 of 2017** | Denies certain building permits where **outstanding charges are owed to the City** on the property | Must be cleared before DOB will issue the PW2 work permit | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll160of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 104 of 2019** | Denies permits on **residential buildings with an excessive number of open violations** | Must confirm the building isn't flagged before filing PW2 (Local Law 104 exception request form exists if this applies) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll104of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 114 of 2019** | Denies permits for certain **occupied buildings** under specified unsafe conditions | Same category of pre-permit check | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll114of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 150 of 2016** | Establishes a **qualification requirement for gas work** | If the ALT2 scope includes any gas piping work, the installer must meet this qualification standard | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll150of2016.pdf |

## Stage 4 — Construction Phase / LPI (Licensing, Permitting, Inspection) Compliance

| Local Law | Subject | Applies to ALT2 when… | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 78 of 2017** | Requires reporting to DOB of any construction incident resulting in **injury or fatality** to a worker or member of the public, within 3 business days | Applies to every active ALT2 construction site — permit holder/contractor/owner obligation | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll78of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 204 of 2017** | Requires **pre-shift safety meetings** for construction workers | Applies to sites requiring Site Safety Training/Plan (same population as LL196) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll204of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 206 of 2017** | Requires **site-specific safety orientations** for workers | Same trigger | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll206of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 203 of 2017** | Sets penalties for violations of **site safety provisions** of the Construction Codes | Applies whenever a site-safety violation is cited during the job | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll203of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 205 of 2017** | Increases civil penalties for construction sites with **excessive violations** | Applies if the site accumulates repeat violations | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll205of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 149 of 2017** | Governs **professionally certified applications** and requires **final inspections of permitted work** | Directly governs the close-out inspection requirement for a Professional-Certification-track ALT2 | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll149of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 151 of 2016** | Requires the **final inspection of gas piping systems** to be performed by DOB in person (with permit holder/RDP/superintendent present); **bans self-certification for gas work** | Any ALT2 scope that installs or alters gas piping | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll151of2016.pdf |
| **Local Law 152 of 2016** | Requires **periodic (4-year cycle) inspection** of exposed gas piping systems, filed via GPS1/GPS2 | Applies to the building generally (not job-specific), but any ALT2 touching gas piping should confirm the building's LL152 status is current | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll152of2016.pdf |
| **Local Law 159 of 2016** | Designates certain gas-piping-system violations as **Immediately Hazardous (Class 1)** | Elevates the enforcement severity of any gas-related violation found during the ALT2 job | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll159of2016.pdf |
| **Local Law 153 of 2016** | Requires owners to notify tenants of correct procedures if a **gas leak** is suspected | Applies to occupied buildings where ALT2 gas work is occurring | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll153of2016.pdf |
| **Local Law 65 of 2018** | Requires **notification of proposed soil or foundation work** | Applies if the ALT2 scope includes any excavation/foundation work | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll65of2018.pdf |
| **Local Law 62 of 2019** | Allows DOB to issue a **Stop Work Order together with a notice to revoke the work permit** | Governs the enforcement mechanics if a serious violation is found mid-construction | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll62of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 157 of 2017** | Increases penalties for **violation of a Stop Work Order** | Same enforcement context | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll157of2017.pdf |
| **Local Law 50 of 2022** | Increases penalties for **failing to certify correction of immediately hazardous conditions**, and mandates re-inspection of those conditions | Governs the Certificate of Correction timeline if a Class 1 (immediately hazardous) violation is issued during the job | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll50of2022.pdf |
| **Local Law 80 of 2021** | Reduces penalties and creates **cure opportunities** for certain violation classes | Relevant to how a violation issued during the job gets resolved before sign-off | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll80of2021.pdf |
| **Local Law 117 of 2019** | Requires DOB/HPD to **audit** a percentage of Certificates of Correction | Background risk factor — a COC filed for a job-related violation can be selected for audit | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll117of2019.pdf |

## Stage 5 — Completion & Sign-Off Compliance

| Local Law | Subject | Applies to ALT2 when… | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 6 of 2021** | Governs **statements of compliance** and the issuance/posting requirements for Certificates of Occupancy and Interim COs | Relevant if the ALT2 nonetheless requires a CO Worksheet/PW6 step (e.g., if occupancy documentation is being refreshed) | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll6of2021.pdf |
| **Local Law 115 of 2019** | Governs **preliminary inspections** | Relevant to scheduling the inspection sequence that precedes sign-off | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll115of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 111 of 2019** | Governs **inspections of construction sites** for which DOB has issued a permit | Same — ties into the final progress/close-out inspection requirement | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll111of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 59 of 2019** | Requires DOB to provide **e-mail notifications for construction project status updates** | Administrative — this is the legal basis for the Job Status email subscription feature used to track sign-off | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll59of2019.pdf |
| **Local Law 149 of 2017** *(again)* | Requires **final inspections of permitted work** before an application can close | Reiterated here because it is the direct legal basis blocking sign-off until final inspection is passed | https://www.nyc.gov/assets/buildings/local_laws/ll149of2017.pdf |

**Reminder from the base workflow:** regardless of which Local Laws apply, DOB will not issue the Letter of Completion / amended CO while **any** open DOB Violation, OATH/ECB summons, or Stop Work Order remains on the job/property — see the enforcement-path document for that mechanism (OATH classes, Certificate of Correction via AEU, SWO rescission).

## Stage 6 — Post-Completion / Ongoing Obligations an ALT2 Can Trigger

These aren't part of the ALT2 filing itself, but an ALT2 that touches certain systems puts the building **on the hook for recurring compliance cycles** afterward. Track these separately:

| Local Law | Recurring Obligation | Triggered by ALT2 scope involving… |
|---|---|---|
| **Local Law 152 of 2016** | Gas piping inspection every 4 years (GPS1/GPS2) | Any new or altered gas piping |
| **Local Law 126 of 2021** | Elevator inspection twice yearly (Category 1/5); parking structure inspection every 4–6 years; parapet inspections | Elevator work; any parking structure component; parapet/exterior work |
| **Local Law 84 of 2009** | Annual energy/water benchmarking | Any system altering the building's energy or water profile |
| **Local Law 87 of 2009** | Periodic energy audit & retro-commissioning | HVAC, lighting, or envelope retrofits |
| **Local Law 97 of 2019** | Annual emissions reporting against building-specific limits (starting 2024) | Any retrofit intended to affect the building's emissions compliance pathway |
| **Local Law 33 of 2018** | Energy Efficiency Grade must be re-posted if underlying benchmarking data changes | System-level energy changes |

## Consolidated "What to Check at Each Stage" Checklist

```
PRE-FILING
  [ ] Is the building pre-1987? → Asbestos investigation (ACP5/ACP7/ACP9) required — LL76/1985, LL37/2009
  [ ] Any occupied dwelling units during construction? → TPP1 required — LL154/2017, LL106/2019, LL118/2019
  [ ] Building/district on the Certification of No Harassment list? → CONH required — LL1/2018, LL140/2021
  [ ] Pre-1960s/78 residential + lead paint condition? → LL40/2021 tenant notification applies

FILING / PLAN REVIEW
  [ ] Scope touches envelope/HVAC/lighting/hot water? → NYCECC energy-code compliance docs (TR8/EN1/EN2) — LL48/2020 / current LL47/2026
  [ ] Structural or specialty scope? → Confirm special inspection agency qualifications under 1 RCNY 101-06 — LL126/2021
  [ ] Filing Professional Certification (POC1/PC1)? → Understand false-certification sanctions — LL105/2019, LL108/2019
  [ ] Is this actually a "gut renovation" (>110% floor area addition)? → Check whether LL154/2021 fossil-fuel rules apply instead of standard ALT2 treatment

PERMIT ISSUANCE
  [ ] Job site size/height trips Site Safety Plan threshold? → SST (LL196/2017) + Construction Superintendent (LL81/2017) required
  [ ] Contractor or property has prior Work-Without-Permit history? → LL155/2017 oversight applies
  [ ] Any outstanding City charges or excessive open violations on the property? → LL160/2017, LL104/2019 permit-denial checks
  [ ] Gas work in scope? → Confirm installer qualification — LL150/2016

CONSTRUCTION / LPI
  [ ] Any injury/fatality on site? → Report within 3 business days — LL78/2017
  [ ] Site Safety Plan site? → Pre-shift meetings (LL204/2017) + site orientations (LL206/2017)
  [ ] Gas piping installed/altered? → No self-certification; in-person DOB final inspection — LL151/2016
  [ ] Excavation/foundation work? → Advance notification required — LL65/2018
  [ ] Violation issued mid-job? → Track class/severity and CURE/cert-of-correction timeline — LL50/2022, LL80/2021

COMPLETION / SIGN-OFF
  [ ] Final inspection of all permitted work completed and passed? — LL149/2017, LL111/2019
  [ ] All violations/SWOs/summonses cleared? (see enforcement-path workflow)
  [ ] CO Worksheet needed (if occupancy documentation involved)? — LL6/2021

POST-COMPLETION (recurring — flag on building's compliance calendar)
  [ ] Gas piping → 4-yr LL152 cycle
  [ ] Elevator/parking/parapet → LL126 cycles
  [ ] Energy systems altered → LL84 benchmarking, LL87 audits, LL97 emissions reporting
```

## Important Caveats

1. **Local Law numbers reset annually.** Always confirm the **year** alongside the number — e.g., "Local Law 154" alone is ambiguous between the 2017 TPP law and the 2021 fossil-fuel law.
2. **Applicability is scope-dependent, not job-type-dependent.** An ALT2 that only touches interior partitions may trigger almost none of these; an ALT2 that touches gas piping, occupied units, structural elements, or building systems can trigger a dozen. Map the **actual scope of work** against this table rather than assuming "ALT2" as a category tells you which laws apply.
3. **Some of these laws amend each other or amend the Building/Administrative Code directly** rather than standing alone — the enacted PDF is the legally authoritative text; DOB's rules (1 RCNY) and service notices explain how DOB implements them operationally. For active project work, verify against the current Construction Codes and DOB's live service notices, not just the original Local Law text, since many have been amended since enactment.
4. **This is not legal advice.** For a specific project, a Registered Design Professional, DOB expediter, or construction attorney should confirm which of these apply to your exact scope of work, building class, and district.
